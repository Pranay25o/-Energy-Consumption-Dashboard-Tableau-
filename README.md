## 📌 Problem Statement

This dashboard helps analyze **energy consumption patterns** across different **countries, regions, and energy sources**.  
It enables stakeholders and analysts to understand how electricity consumption (KWH) and consumption standard units (CSU) vary globally.

Through this dashboard, users can:
- Identify high and low energy-consuming countries
- Compare regional energy usage
- Analyze contribution of renewable energy sources
- Support data-driven decisions for energy planning and sustainability

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

- Format: CSV  
- Type: Public / Sample dataset  
- Metrics:
  - KWH (Kilowatt Hours)
  - CSU (Consumption Standard Units)
- Dimensions:
  - Country
  - Region
  - Energy Source (Biomass, Geothermal, Hydro, Solar, Wind)

## 📈 Insights

Following insights can be derived from the dashboard:

### 🔹 [1] KWH by Country
- Countries show significant variation in energy consumption
- Some countries consume nearly **2–3x more energy** than others

### 🔹 [2] CSU by Country
- CSU values help normalize energy usage comparison
- Countries with moderate KWH sometimes show high CSU values

### 🔹 [3] KWH by Region
- Europe and South America show relatively higher energy usage
- Africa shows comparatively lower energy consumption

### 🔹 [4] CSU by Region
- Regional CSU distribution indicates energy demand imbalance
- North America and Europe show stable consumption patterns

### 🔹 [5] Energy Source Analysis
- **Wind and Hydro** contribute the highest KWH
- **Geothermal** has the lowest consumption across metrics
- Renewable sources play a major role in overall energy supply
